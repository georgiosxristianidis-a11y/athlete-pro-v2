Athlete Pro is a push / pull / legs training log with an optional AI coach. Publisher contact: georgiosxristianidis@gmail.com. By opening the app or these pages you accept these terms. If you do not, do not use the service.

1. What this is not

This is not medical care, a doctor’s prescription, or a personal-training contract. Estimated 1RMs, BMI, muscle-fatigue scores and AI-coach text are calculations and drafts from your own log. They can be incomplete or wrong. If you have health doubts before loading, talk to a clinician, not to the app.

2. How you may use it

  • The log runs in the browser and as an installed PWA. In airgap mode (the default for a new install) workouts do not leave the device.
  • The cloud replica and the AI coach are separate opt-ins. AI is off on a new install. If you paste your own Anthropic or Gemini key (BYOK), it stays in on-device settings and is not synced to the cloud replica; a coach request sends it to our server and then to the selected engine.
  • Publisher-side keys, when configured, may also serve a request. /api/coach is limited to 10 requests per minute per address; the broader API limit is 100 requests per 15 minutes. Hitting the cap is a refusal, not an unlimited chat.
  • Do not try to bypass those limits, interfere with other people’s data, plant hostile exercise names or prompts, or pass a model reply off as professional advice.

3. Your records

What you log is your training content. The publisher does not claim ownership of your sets. You can export JSON from the data passport. The in-app delete button does not clear all seven stores — details are in the privacy policy.
