The verdict

The most dangerous systemic issues have been eliminated. The structural separation of Brand and Semantic colors is elite. However, the system is still haunted by architectural residue in the form of !important tags and an empty documentation spec.

The conflicting "Vantablack" documentation was successfully moved to the archive. The missing --c-surface-deep token that was breaking rendering in production has been surgically removed. The radius scale has been updated. The app is much healthier, but 7 dead typography tokens in the Athlete Room and scattered !important hacks prevent this from being a truly flawless A-tier system.
